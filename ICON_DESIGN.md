# DiskSweep App Icon

DiskSweep's icon uses a compact brushed-aluminium internal drive as the central silhouette. A single aqua sweep curves around the drive and two restrained sparkles communicate cleanup without suggesting antivirus software or destructive deletion.

The deep navy-to-teal rounded tile matches the application's cool accent palette. Large shapes, high edge contrast, and minimal surface detail keep the mark readable down to the 16-point menu and Finder sizes.

The asset catalog contains the complete macOS set from 16×16 through 512×512 at 1× and 2×. The generated source artwork is stored at `Artwork/DiskSweepIcon-Master.png`.

Generation constraints: no text, brand marks, broom, trash can, shield, malware imagery, or watermark.
